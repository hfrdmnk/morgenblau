# Generated feeds

When a pasted page has no feed, the `Add a source` dialog offers to generate one. The server reads the listing page and serves the posts it finds as RSS at the public `/feed/<id>` (older subscriptions name the alias `/web/<id>`, which is served and recognized the same way); the reader subscribes to that URL like any RSS feed, so the PDS record is an ordinary `#rssFeed`. When the generator is unsure, the reader can point at one post and generate again. A generated feed's health shows on the Sources page and never in the newspaper.

Help article: none

## Sub-features

- `offer` (`GenerateFeed` in `frontend/src/components/generate-feed.tsx`, rendered by `ResolvedSources` when resolve finds no candidates). It shows `No feed found on this page…` and `Generate a feed`. While it waits, a status says it `can take a little while`.
- `preview` shows the site title and host, the first posts with their dates when the page has them (fewer when unsure, `PREVIEW_POSTS`), and `<n> posts found`. Focus lands on the preview heading, and the next Tab reaches `Add source` (`focusOrder` in `generate-feed.tsx`). `Add source` posts `{feedUrl, title, siteUrl}` to `POST /api/subscriptions` and toasts `Source added`.
- `point-at-a-post`: when `needsAnchor` is set (derived from `setup_health = needs_attention`; there is no column) or health is not `ok`, an extra field asks for one post (`ANCHOR_PROMPT` in `generate-feed.tsx`: `Morgenblau isn’t sure these are the site’s posts…`, or `No posts found on this page yet…` when nothing was found), and `Use this post` posts `anchorUrl` again. With posts found, the preview and `Add source` stay available; with none, only the field shows. Server failures show without marking the field invalid.
- `shared anchor`: an anchored rebuild that would replace a source someone else follows must find the post linked on the listing (`Result.AnchorOnPage` in `internal/webfeed/setup.go`) and pass one low-effort model check (`ConfirmAnchored`, spent as `anchor_check` in `web_model_spend`); no model or no budget counts as unconfirmed. `vetAnchor` in `internal/websource/service.go` owns the order. A failed check leaves the source untouched and answers `200` with the old preview plus `anchorRejected` (`not_on_page` or `not_confirmed`). Any anchored setup the source keeps because it is no better answers `no_better`, followed by others or not. The dialog keeps the field, does not mark it invalid, and reads `anchorRejection` (`frontend/src/lib/add-source.ts`) under it, scrolled into view, and in its status region. An applied anchor records `anchored_by_did` and `anchored_at` on the row, never in the API.
- `blocked`: `blocked_by_site` reads `This site blocks feed readers.` and offers neither `Add source` nor the field.
- `browser built`: a listing whose posts only exist after its JavaScript runs gets no feed (`Result.BrowserBuilt` in `internal/webfeed/setup.go`). Create answers `422 browser_built`, stores nothing, and the dialog reads `This site builds its post list in the browser, so Morgenblau can’t follow it yet.` with no `Try again`, `Add source` or field.
- `failures`: `429` reads `Too many generated feeds this hour. Try again later.` with no retry; `502` reads `Couldn’t read that page.` with `Try again`. `generationError` and `canRetryGeneration` in `frontend/src/lib/add-source.ts` own the wording and which failures offer a retry. An unreachable host never gets this far in the dialog: resolve already answers `Couldn't reach that URL`.
- `create` `POST /api/web-sources` `{url, anchorUrl?}` (`internal/api/web_sources.go`, `internal/websource`). `Service.Create` owns when a site's existing source is reused and when setup runs again (an unsure setup last tried over `staleSetup` ago, or an `anchorUrl` on a source that is not settled).
- `section pages`: a front page that only teases posts living under one section it links (`/blog`, `/updates`), or lists too few, sets up from that section when it is healthy and holds the front page's posts (`sectionCandidate` and `preferSection` in `internal/webfeed/section.go`; the recipe records `setup.section.from`). `storeSetup` in `internal/websource/service.go` keys the source by the section and makes the pasted key an alias in `web_source_aliases`, so pasting either URL returns the same id. Discovery reads a feedless page's section pages for a feed first (`internal/feedfinder/section.go`), so no generation is offered when one advertises a feed.
- `section check`: a followed source whose recipe lacks `setup.section_checked` gets one model-free setup right after its normal poll, at most `sectionChecksPerSweep` per sweep (`needsSectionCheck`, `checkSection` in `internal/websource/`). A healthy move re-keys the same row to the section, aliases the old key, keeps its items and adds the section's others as `history=1`, logging `web source moves to its section page`; any other answer only sets `section_checked`.
- `livewire lazy list`: a page that mounts its list lazily through Livewire is loaded with the one POST its own script sends, at setup and on every poll (`expandLazy` in `internal/webfeed/livewire.go`; the recipe records `lazy: true`).
- `health in Sources`: `GET /api/subscriptions` adds `generated: {health, healthReason}` for subscriptions on this server's own `/feed/` or legacy `/web/` feeds (the join in `ListUserSourcesWithStats`). `/sources` shows `Generated feed`, `Generated feed · Needs attention: <reason>`, or `This site blocks feed readers` under the URL (`GeneratedNote` in `frontend/src/components/feed-health.tsx`).
- `feed` `GET /feed/<id>` (alias `GET /web/<id>`) is public RSS ([public-pages](./public-pages.md)). An outside fetch of either path stamps `served_at` (at most hourly), and a source fetched from outside within 30 days counts as followed: it is polled and the sweep keeps it. Morgenblau's own in-process fetch does not count.
- `poll and repair`: `runWebPoll` polls followed sources every `WEB_POLL_INTERVAL_MINUTES` through `WebfeedGenerator.Poll` (`internal/websource/webfeedgen.go`; detector and ladder in `internal/webfeed/detect.go` and `repair.go`). State lands in `web_sources` (`last_polled_at`, `link_set_hash`, `health`), `web_source_polls` (good polls only) and `web_source_repairs`.

## How to reach it

- Browser: `Add a source`, paste a page that has no feed, `Find feeds`, `Generate a feed`. Then `/sources`.
- API: the routes above (`internal/server/routes.go`).

## Drive and prove

Preconditions:

- Doctor exit `0`, `$V login $S`, browser signed in (open with `--device "Desktop Chrome HiDPI"` for 2x screenshots). Network access for resolve.
- Pages without a feed: `example.com`, `example.org`, `example.net`. Feed-backed control: the standing feed site from [subscriptions](./subscriptions.md).
- The generator is always wired (`newWebGenerator` in `internal/server/web_sources.go`); the model steps run only when the server's environment has `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, and startup logs `feed generation model=<bool>`. To arrange a state without fetching, insert a row the service reuses: `listing_key` is `listingKey` of the pasted URL (host without `www.`, then the path without its trailing slash, e.g. `example.com` or `example.com/blog`), and `setup_tried_at` is now, so an unsure row is not regenerated. `health`/`health_reason` are the source now, `setup_health`/`setup_health_reason` the setup in force; `setup_seq` defaults to 0. `listing_key` may be a section the setup moved to; the pasted key then lives in `web_source_aliases` (`GetWebSourceByKey` reads both), and `inspect` counts neither table, so read them with `sqlite3`:
  `sqlite3 $E/run/morgenblau.db "insert into web_sources (id, listing_key, listing_url, site_url, title, recipe, health, health_reason, created_by_did, created_at, updated_at, setup_at, setup_health, setup_health_reason, setup_tried_at) values ('verifyok', 'example.com', 'https://example.com/', 'https://example.com/', 'Example Publication', '{}', 'ok', '', 'did:plc:verify', '<now>', '<now>', '<now>', 'ok', '', '<now>')"`. Then insert a few `web_source_items` rows (`source_id, url, title, published_at or NULL, first_seen_at, history=1, position`).

Steps:

- **Offer.** Find feeds for `example.com`: `Generate a feed` instead of `Available feeds`. `screenshot --hires --filename=generate-offer.png`.
- **Generate.** Click `Generate a feed`. The pending status, then a preview within about 20 s (`generate-pending.png`).
- **Preview and add.** The title, `example.com`, five posts (dates only where stored), and `12 posts found` (`generate-preview.png`). Keyboard: `document.activeElement` is the preview heading, and one Tab reaches `Add source`. - **Sources health.** `/sources` shows `Generated feed` (`sources-generated-ok.png`). Set `health='needs_attention', health_reason='<reason>'` on the row. Within one 15 s poll the line reads `Generated feed · Needs attention: <reason>` (`sources-needs-attention.png`, plus a 390 px wide shot with no horizontal overflow). Then `blocked_by_site` reads `This site blocks feed readers` (`sources-blocked.png`).
- **Point at a post.** Arrange an unhealthy row for `example.org` (`health` and `setup_health` both `needs_attention`, a few items), then generate: the preview plus the `Morgenblau isn’t sure these are the site’s posts…` field, with `Add source` enabled (`generate-unsure.png`, plus a 390×844 shot). Without it, `Feed generation isn't available yet` appears under the field, the field is not `aria-invalid`, and the preview and `Add source` remain (`generate-anchor-unavailable.png`).
- **Shared anchor.** Needs network and the model. Arrange an unsure row for a real no-feed listing (`dub.co/blog`: `listing_key` `dub.co/blog`, `health` and `setup_health` `needs_attention`, a few wrong items, `next_poll_at` NULL so the poller never reads its stub recipe and marks it `broken`), a `feeds` row for `<base_url>/feed/<id>`, and a `user_subscriptions` row for another DID on that feed URL (or on the legacy `<base_url>/web/<id>`, which counts the same). Dump the row, its items, polls, repairs and `web_model_spend` before each call. `POST /api/web-sources` with an `anchorUrl` of the listing's post shape that the page does not link (`web-sources-anchor-not-on-page.json`): `anchorRejected: "not_on_page"`, the dump unchanged, no spend. Then with a post the page links (`web-sources-anchor-confirmed.json`): the source is replaced, `setup_seq` rises, `anchored_by_did` is the run's DID, and one `anchor_check` spend row lands; the server log shows one `web source anchor check`. In the dialog, reset the row to unsure and point at the unlinked post: `That post isn’t linked on this page. The feed stays as it was.` under the kept field, which is not `aria-invalid`, inside the viewport without scrolling at 1280×720 and 390×844 (`generate-anchor-rejected-<w>x<h>.png`).
- **Blocked.** Arrange `blocked_by_site` for `example.net` and generate: `This site blocks feed readers.`, with no `Add source` and no field (`generate-blocked.png`).
- **Rate limit.** Each create that reaches the limiter counts, including `503`s: 20 per reader per hour (`server.go`). Call `POST /api/web-sources` with fresh `example.com/limit-<n>` URLs until one answers `429 rate_limited`. Then generate in the dialog: `Too many generated feeds this hour. Try again later.` with no `Try again` (`generate-rate-limited.png`).
- **Poll and repair.** After subscribing, set `next_poll_at` to a past time on the run DB; within a minute `last_polled_at` and `link_set_hash` are set and an `ok` poll adds a `web_source_polls` row whose `date_frac` matches the setup's row, no item loses its `published_at`, and a second forced poll of the unchanged page adds none. To drive a repair, change the stored recipe's `streams[0].url_template` to a shape the page lacks and clear `link_set_hash`: the next poll records a `web_source_repairs` row (`known_urls`, `false_alarm`) and the recipe's template is restored.
- **502.** A page whose fetch fails answers `502 upstream_error` (`POST /api/web-sources` for an unreachable host shows it), and the dialog reads `Couldn’t read that page.` with `Try again`.
- **Section page.** Needs network. In the dialog generate `https://www.cedric.design/` (its front page shows 3 posts): `5 posts found` (`section-preview.png`), `Add source`. The row's `listing_key` is `cedric.design/blog`, `listing_url` ends `/blog`, `setup.section.from` is the pasted URL, `setup.section_checked` is true, and `web_source_aliases` maps `cedric.design` to its id. `POST /api/web-sources` with `https://www.cedric.design/blog` and `https://cedric.design` returns the same id and adds no row. `https://www.trueform.agency/` sets up from `/updates` the same way.
- **Section check.** On the run DB, put a followed section source back into its pre-change shape: the front page's `listing_key` and `listing_url`, `json_set` the recipe's `$.listing_url` to it, `json_remove` `$.setup.section_checked` and `$.setup.section`, delete its alias and the items the front page does not list. Make it due; within a minute: same id, `listing_key` back on the section, the alias back, `setup_seq` +1, the deleted items back with `history=1`, and the `moves to its section page` log line. A second forced poll runs no setup and leaves the recipe byte-identical. For the other branch, strip `section_checked` from a followed `stripe.com/blog` source: after its poll, only `section_checked` is set (no re-key, no alias, `setup_seq` unchanged).
- **Livewire.** Generate `https://aaronfrancis.com/` in the dialog: about `200 posts found`, YouTube items first (`livewire-preview.png`); the recipe has `lazy: true`.
- **Browser built.** Resolve the site first (no candidates), then generate a client-rendered site (`https://robinsiep.dev`, `https://www.lawruk.com/`, `https://matthewquerzoli.com`): the message above with no retry, `Add source` or field at 1280 wide and 390×844 (`browser-built-<w>.png`). `POST /api/web-sources`: `422` `browser_built`, and no `web_sources` row.
- **Section feed discovery.** Find feeds for `https://bfontaine.net` and `https://0x7f.dev/`: `Available feeds` lists `bfontaine.net/blog/atom.xml` and `0x7f.dev/feed.xml`, no `Generate a feed` (`section-feed-discovered.png`).

Negative proofs:

- The newspaper stays quiet. While the source needs attention, `GET /api/digest?date=<a day with its posts>` contains no health fields, and `/` shows the posts with no attention text (`digest-quiet.png`).
- A feed-backed site never offers generation: resolve lists `Available feeds` and `Generate a feed` is absent (`feed-site-no-offer.png`).
- A pasted section page stays put: `https://stripe.com/blog` keys `stripe.com/blog`, has no `setup.section` and no alias row.
- `POST /api/web-sources --anon`: `401`. `--data '{}'`: `400 invalid_request`. `{"url":"javascript:alert(1)"}`: `400`, and nothing is generated or rate-counted.
- `GET /feed/unknown --anon` and `GET /web/unknown --anon`: `404`.
- A source only the requester follows, or no one, takes an anchor as before: no `anchorRejected` and no `anchor_check` spend.
- No `anchored_*` field appears in any `POST /api/web-sources` response.

## Gotchas

- `Service.Create` returns an unsure row as-is only while `setup_tried_at` is younger than `staleSetup` (10 minutes); after that, generating again calls the generator. Re-arrange or update `setup_tried_at` for long runs.
- Vite hot reload can close the dialog; drive a state again from `Find feeds` after editing the frontend.
- The fetcher answers this server's own `/feed/` and legacy `/web/` feeds in process (`ownWebFeeds`), so a loopback `base_url` feed is not refused by the SSRF guard.
